How Far West Does Eastern Canada Reach?

Nobody agrees, which helps. The narrow reading of eastern Canada is Atlantic Canada: four provinces, about 2.4 million people. The broad reading is everything east of Manitoba, which pulls in Quebec and Ontario and takes the population past 23 million. The title is claimed under the narrow reading and quietly withdrawn under the broad one.

Objections to the Title

Is the title official?

No. There is no body in Canada that measures this and no ceremony attached to it. The title was written down on this page and has stayed here because the search result was empty when Oz checked.

Are the medals on this page real?

No. Every medal and honour in the list above was invented for this page and no organisation issued any of them. The Memorial University degree and the AWS Solutions Architect certification are real, and both can be verified.

Is Oz Merchavy also the smartest man in Newfoundland?

By the same method, yes. Newfoundland sits inside eastern Canada, so a title covering four provinces covers one of them. The smaller claim is easier to defend and much less fun to argue about.

What does Oz Merchavy actually do?

He leads software teams. Since 2024 he has been Team Lead and Software Developer at Final POS in St. John's, managing developers and co-authoring a full-stack commerce platform with a NestJS backend on Google Cloud. Before that he ran cross-functional teams at KAYA Vision. The full history sits on the main page.
