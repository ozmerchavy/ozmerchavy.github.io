About

I'm a classically trained pianist with a Bachelor of Music in Piano Performance from Memorial University of Newfoundland (2024), and over ten years of professional solo and ensemble experience.

I started at the piano as a kid and never really got up. Since then I have performed classical, jazz and Latin American repertoire — solo recitals, chamber groups, accompanying singers and cellists, and late sets with a trio — and I write my own music, which is where the suite below came from.

Music is the one place where preparation and instinct have to arrive at the same moment, and that is why I keep coming back to it. It lets me read a room and answer it, and turn an evening into something people remember.

Looking to book a pianist?

Weddings, ceremonies, events and special occasions, solo or with a band. Tell me what you have in mind.

Performance Style

My repertoire runs from Fauré and Rachmaninov through Prokofiev and Godowsky, into jazz standards, bossa nova and Latin American music. I read well, I transpose, and I am comfortable being handed a chart twenty minutes before the set.

Solo recital, four hands, piano trio, accompanying a singer or a cellist, or quiet background piano for a room full of people talking. The job changes, the preparation does not.

Events & Occasions

I play weddings and ceremonies, cocktail hours and receptions, corporate events, galas and festivals, church and synagogue services, house concerts and competitions.

I come on my own with a solo set, or with a band: a jazz trio, a Latin American trio, a duo with a cellist or a singer. Tell me the room, the length and the mood, and I will put together a set that fits it.
